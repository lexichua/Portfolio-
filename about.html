About Resume

Hi I’m Lexi!

I grew up in the SF Bay Area and I am currently studying Cognitive Science at UCLA class of 29’.

I am interested in what drives people’s thoughts, feelings, and interactions, and I strive to design experiences that seamlessly integrate the way we think.

Growing up, I loved video game art and storytelling which inspired me to create art of my own. I started making digital art when I was 11 and it has been a part of my life since!
